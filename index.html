StillSend

Privacy Policy · Effective October 10, 2026

StillSend is a local-first climbing training and workout tracker. This policy explains what the Android app stores, why optional Android access may be requested, and how you can control your information.

Information stored on your device

StillSend may store the information you enter or generate while using the app, including:

  • climbing sessions, grades, outcomes, attempts, dates, durations, and rest time;
  • hangboard, warm-up, and recovery workouts;
  • workout notes, custom routines, routine edits, and exercise loads;
  • XP, level, rank, comfort-grade, and recap calculations;
  • optional settings such as body weight, home gym, visible grades, selected theme, chime preferences, and selected media app; and
  • active-workout and timer state needed to keep a workout together.

The current version does not create an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or upload this information to a StillSend server. The app does not currently collect heart-rate data.

Optional Android access

StillSend can be used without the following optional access:

  • Notification access: Android grants broad notification-listener access. StillSend uses it only to ask Android for the active media session from the media app you selected so the current title and artist can be displayed. StillSend does not save or transmit notification content.
  • Notifications and Do Not Disturb access: If you enable DND bypass, StillSend uses these permissions only to deliver a short rest-complete alert. StillSend does not change your DND schedule or other apps' settings.
  • FocusOS theme access: When FocusOS is installed and theme following is enabled, StillSend reads only the selected FocusOS palette through its local theme provider.

Manual backups

You can export a StillSend backup through Android's system file picker and choose where it is saved. A backup contains your StillSend workout databases and settings. The backup is not uploaded by StillSend and is not encrypted by the app, so store and share it carefully.

StillSend excludes its workout databases and preferences from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Manual export and import are the supported ways to move existing StillSend records between installations or signing versions.

Retention and deletion

Information remains on your device until you delete an individual workout, use Erase all StillSend data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Erasing all data removes StillSend's local databases and preferences from the current installation. Copies you exported elsewhere are not deleted automatically.

Data sharing

StillSend does not sell personal information. The current version does not send workout information to StillSend, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties. Android, your device manufacturer, a media app, FocusOS, or a storage provider you select may process information under their own policies when you use their system features.

Fitness information

StillSend is a training journal, not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Its estimates, reminders, and workout guidance are for general fitness information and are not medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and stop training if you experience pain or other concerning symptoms.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before any release that materially changes how StillSend handles information, including a future optional heart-rate feature or online account.
